# Timora

Timora is an academic calendar and productivity app for students. It provides a full-featured calendar with day/week/month/year views, event management (CRUD), upcoming deadline tracking, study partner coordination, and preparation checklists.

## Features

- **Calendar Views**: View your schedule in day, week, month, or year formats.
- **Event Management**: Create, read, update, and delete events with ease.
- **Deadline Tracking**: Keep track of upcoming deadlines and receive notifications.
- **Study Partner Coordination**: Connect with classmates to coordinate study sessions.
- **Preparation Checklists**: Create checklists to stay organized and on top of your tasks
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Intuitive design for easy navigation and use.
- **Cross-Platform Compatibility**: Available on multiple platforms for seamless access.
- **Data Security**: Your data is securely stored and protected.
- **Customizable Notifications**: Set reminders for important events and deadlines.
- **Integration with Academic Systems**: Sync with your school's calendar and systems for real-time updates.
- **Analytics and Insights**: Get insights into your study habits and productivity patterns.
